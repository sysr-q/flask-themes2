# Contributors

Flask-Themes2 builds on the work of several projects and many contributors:

- **[Flask-Themes](https://bitbucket.org/leafstorm/flask-themes)** - the original
  extension, created by Matthew "LeafStorm" Frazier.
- **[Spirits](https://github.com/drewlustro/spirits)** - a fork of Flask-Themes,
  maintained by Drew Lustro.
- **[Flask-Themes2](https://github.com/sysr-q/flask-themes2)** - a fork of Spirits,
  maintained by Chris Carter with (enormous) help from the community.
